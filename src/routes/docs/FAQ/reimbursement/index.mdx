# Client Reimbursements

## What are reimbursements?
As a nonprofit, Birdflop periodically reimburses clients based on excess profit. These reimbursements are usage-dependent and designed to maximally lower prices at high service utilization. All reimbursements are issued as credit to client accounts.

## How do they work?
- **Frequency**: Reimbursements are generally announced and distributed quarterly
- **Rate variability**: Reimbursement rates vary from period to period and can range from 0% to 100%
- **Calculation basis**: Reimbursements are calculated as a percentage of external transactions from each quarter
- **Geographic rates**: Transactions are categorized into US reimbursement rates, EU reimbursement rates ,or a combination based on the client's plans
- **Exclusions**: Internal credits/payments made using past reimbursement credits are not eligible for reimbursement

## Who is eligible?
Active clients are eligible for reimbursements. Active clients are defined as users who have at least one active service (not canceled or suspended) hosted on Birdflop at the time of reimbursement distribution.

## Timeline
- **Rate announcement**: Reimbursement rates are announced within 15 days of the end of each reimbursement period
- **Distribution**: All eligible clients receive their reimbursements within 31 days of the end of the reimbursement period
- **Payment method**: All reimbursements are issued as credit to client accounts
